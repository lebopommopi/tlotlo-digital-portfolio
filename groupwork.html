TEAMWORK & PERSONAL GROWTH ♡

My Group Work Experience

Reflecting on teamwork, communication and collaboration through Tuckman's five stages of group development.

About Our Group Project

During my first year at university, I participated in a group assignment for the Fundamentals of Business Finance module. Our task involved extracting financial statements for a financial year.

This experience gave me an opportunity to work with other students, share responsibilities and learn about the importance of communication and cooperation when completing academic tasks.

01

Forming Stage

Getting to Know Our Team

Our group consisted entirely of female students, and I already knew my group members before we started working together. This familiarity made it easier for us to begin communicating and discussing the assignment.

At this stage, we needed to understand the requirements of our Fundamentals of Business Finance project and determine how we would approach the task as a team.

02

Storming Stage

Facing Challenges Together

As we worked on the assignment, we experienced challenges with poor communication and time management. These difficulties affected our progress and resulted in us rushing to complete some tasks at the last minute.

This stage made me realise that knowing your group members does not automatically guarantee effective teamwork. Clear communication, planning and accountability are necessary for a group to function successfully.

03

Norming Stage

Building Better Cooperation

To improve our teamwork, we started using Microsoft Teams to hold meetings and communicate about the project. We divided responsibilities according to each member's strengths and abilities, shared ideas and agreed on deadlines to encourage everyone to contribute.

We also made an effort to listen to one another, respect different opinions and assist members who were struggling. These changes helped us become more organised and work towards our common goal.

04

Performing Stage

Working Towards Success

As our teamwork improved, we became more effective in completing our responsibilities. Everyone contributed to the assignment and fulfilled their assigned tasks.

Our overall performance was excellent because we supported one another and encouraged each other to remain focused. Having a strong support system helped us work together more confidently and complete our project successfully.

05

Adjourning Stage

Completing Our Journey

Our group project ended with a successful presentation. Reaching this final stage was an important achievement because we had managed to complete the assignment despite the communication and time-management challenges we faced earlier.

Looking back on the experience, I appreciate the effort each member contributed and the support we provided to one another throughout the project.

What I Learned From This Experience ♡

Working in this group taught me valuable lessons about communication, cooperation, patience and teamwork. I learned that successful group work requires more than simply dividing tasks. It also involves listening to others, respecting different perspectives and supporting one another when challenges arise.

One important lesson I will carry into future group assignments is the need to communicate consistently and manage time more effectively. Although our project ended successfully, I believe that better planning from the beginning would have reduced the pressure we experienced towards the deadline.

This experience has helped me understand how effective collaboration can contribute to academic success and prepare me for future professional environments.
